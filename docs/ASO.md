**Description**

After eye surgery you may need four different drops, several times a day, for weeks, and the schedule changes every week. DropClock turns your doctor's instructions into reminders that ring on time.

WHY PEOPLE SWITCH TO DROPCLOCK
• Reminders that actually ring. We keep your alarms refreshed so iOS never silently drops them.
• Taper schedules in one step. Enter "4 times a day for 1 week, then 3, then 2" once, and DropClock handles every day after that.
• Left eye and right eye, separately. Perfect when your second cataract surgery is a few weeks after the first.
• Automatic 5-minute spacing. When two drops are due at the same time, DropClock spaces them out for you.
• Recognize drops by cap color. Each drop shows the color of its bottle cap.
• Mark as taken from the notification. Take a drop early and that reminder stops.
• History for your eye doctor. See every drop you took and share it at your next visit.

MADE FOR
• Cataract, LASIK, PRK, and corneal surgery recovery
• Glaucoma and other daily, long-term drops
• Caregivers managing drops for a parent or partner
• Pets on eye drops after a vet visit

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no server. Your data stays on your iPhone.

PRICING
Free for one drop. Unlock unlimited drops, both-eye schedules, and tapers with a single one-time purchase. No subscription.

DropClock is a reminder tool and does not provide medical advice. Always follow your doctor's instructions.

**説明文**

白内障やレーシックの手術後は、何種類もの目薬を1日に何度も、しかも週ごとに回数を変えながら点眼する必要があります。DropClockは、医師の指示どおりのスケジュールを入力するだけで、毎回きちんとお知らせします。

DropClockが選ばれる理由
・ちゃんと鳴るアラーム：通知が消えてしまわないよう、自動で更新し続けます。
・減量スケジュールに対応：「1週目は1日4回、2週目は3回、3週目は2回」を一度入力するだけ。
・左目・右目を別々に管理：片目ずつ数週間あけて手術する場合にも対応。
・目薬の間隔を自動で5分あける：同じ時間に重なった目薬は自動でずらします。
・キャップの色で見分ける：目薬ごとにキャップの色を表示します。
・通知から「点眼済み」：早めに点眼した回の通知は止まります。
・点眼記録：通院時に医師に見せられる記録を残せます。

こんな方に
・白内障・レーシック・PRKなどの術後ケア
・緑内障など、毎日続ける点眼
・ご家族の点眼を管理する方
・ペットの目薬管理
