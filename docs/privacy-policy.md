# DropClock Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

## English

DropClock does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- Everything you enter (eye drops, schedules, check-offs, eye pressure readings, notes) is stored only on your device.
- DropClock has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.
- Reminders are delivered with your iPhone's local notifications. Nothing is sent over the internet.
- If you use "PDF report" or share your history, the file is created on your device and sent only where you choose (for example, Mail or AirDrop).
- Deleting the app deletes all of its data. If you use iCloud device backups, DropClock data may be included in those backups under Apple's privacy policy.

DropClock is a reminder tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always follow your doctor's instructions.
